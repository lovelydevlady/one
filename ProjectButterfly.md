## 1. Executive Summary
**Project Butterfly** is a modular runtime system designed to **securely deliver, manage, and execute packages** while remaining **privacy-first and extensible**.  

- It demonstrates a **new paradigm of software delivery**.  
- Packages are **encrypted and verified before execution**.  
- Runtime communicates internally through **dynamic task queues and message buses**.
